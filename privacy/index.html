Privacy Policy

Composio Buffer Integration · Last updated: August 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Composio Buffer Integration handles information when a user authorizes the integration to connect a Buffer account with supported automation services.

1. Information We Access

After authorization, the integration may access information necessary to provide the requested Buffer functionality, including Buffer account information, connected social channels, and content or scheduling information needed to create, manage, schedule, or publish posts.

2. How Information Is Used

Information accessed through the integration is used only to provide the Buffer management and automation functionality requested by the user. We do not sell personal information or use connected account information for unrelated advertising purposes.

3. Buffer Authorization

Authorization is performed through Buffer's authentication system. Users can revoke access through the relevant Buffer account settings or by discontinuing authorization of the integration.

4. Data Sharing

Information is not sold or rented to third parties. Information may be processed by service providers required to operate the integration and fulfill the functionality requested by the user.

5. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information processed through the integration. No Internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the requested functionality or to satisfy applicable legal, security, and operational requirements.

7. User Choices and Revocation

Users may revoke the integration's access through Buffer or other connected-service settings. Revoking authorization prevents the integration from continuing to access the revoked account connection.

8. Children's Privacy

The integration is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the integration's functionality or applicable requirements change. The latest version will always be published at this address.

10. Contact

For privacy questions concerning this integration, contact the application administrator through the service or platform through which the integration was provided.
